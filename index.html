Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 24-02-2025

1. About Find My Bogie

Find My Bogie is a train organization and tracking app designed to help users locate train bogies based on real-time data entered by a Train Master. The app operates with two roles:

  • Train Master: Responsible for entering the arrangement of train bogies.
  • User: Can view the bogie arrangements entered by the Train Master.

To facilitate smooth data exchange, we use Google Firebase to store and manage train arrangement data securely.

2. Information We Collect

2.1. Personal Information (Optional)

Users may create an account by providing a name and email address. Alternatively, users can skip account creation and use the app without providing personal information.

2.2. Train Bogie Data

The Train Master enters train bogie arrangements, which are stored in Firebase. Regular users can view the stored train bogie data.

2.3. Automatically Collected Information

We may collect the following non-personal data:

  • App Usage Data: Information about how users interact with the app to improve performance.
  • Device Information: Basic device details (e.g., device type, operating system) to optimize app functionality.

Note: We do not collect sensitive personal data such as financial information, location tracking, or biometric data.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data for the following purposes:

  • To enable the Train Master to enter and manage train bogie arrangements.
  • To allow Users to view the arrangement of train bogies.
  • To enhance user experience by analyzing app performance.
  • To display advertisements through Google AdMob.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties.

4. Data Storage & Security

All stored data is securely managed in Google Firebase, a trusted cloud database provider. We take reasonable security measures to prevent unauthorized access to user data. However, no online platform is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute data protection.

5. Advertisements & Third-Party Services

Find My Bogie displays advertisements through Google AdMob, which may collect and use data to provide personalized ads.

Google AdMob Privacy Policy: Google’s Privacy Policy

Users can manage ad preferences through Google’s Ad Settings.

Note: We do not control the data collection practices of Google AdMob.

6. Your Choices & User Rights

We respect your right to control your data. You can:

  • Use the app without creating an account (Guest Mode).
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data (if applicable).
  • Manage your ad preferences via Google’s settings.

To make a request regarding your data, please contact us at info@letdigitalfly.com.

7. Children's Privacy

Find My Bogie is not intended for children under 13 years of age. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided personal information, we will delete it immediately.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted within the app, and we encourage users to review it regularly.

If major changes occur, we will notify users through an in-app update or email.
